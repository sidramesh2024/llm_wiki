# IKE-UW-322 Coastal named storm

## Rule

- Within **20 miles** of the Atlantic or Gulf coast: named-storm deductible
  **2%** of TIV, minimum $100,000.
- Wind sublimit unless construction is masonry non-combustible or better.
- Frame construction in this band is a decline over $10M.
