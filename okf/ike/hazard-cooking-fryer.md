# IKE-UW-210 Cooking and fryer lines

Stacks with [IKE-UW-110 Food processing occupancy](occupancy-food-processing.md).

## Rule

- Wet-chemical or water-mist over fryers, plus listed hood suppression.
- Semi-annual cleaning records required.
- Any fryer line over 2M BTU is automatic referral even if TIV is within
  occupancy limits.
