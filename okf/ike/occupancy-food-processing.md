# IKE-UW-110 Food processing occupancy

Applies to snack, frozen, canned, and packaged-food manufacturing.

## Appetite

- Target: sprinklered, FM-approved cooking protection, recall plan on file.
- Maximum without referral: **$30M** TIV at a single food-processing location.
- Combined food-processing schedule without referral: **$75M**.

## Referral / decline

- Referral: any location with deep-fat frying, ammonia refrigeration, or unsprinklered processing.
- Decline: uninsured product-recall exposure above $5M with no crisis vendor.
