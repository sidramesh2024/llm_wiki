# IKE-UW-118 Life sciences occupancy

Applies to medical device, diagnostics, and biopharma manufacturing including
clean rooms and validated utilities.

## Appetite

- Target: sprinklered campuses, redundant utilities, documented BI worksheet.
- Maximum without referral: **$100M** TIV per campus.
- Business interruption: require 18-month period of indemnity for sterile fill
  or unique validated lines.

## Referral / decline

- Referral: single-source sterile capacity, or BI values above 40% of TIV.
- Decline: unvalidated contract-manufacturing with no COPE.
