# IKE-UW-124 Warehouse occupancy

Applies to finished-goods distribution, 3PL, and dry or refrigerated warehouse.

## Appetite

- Target: masonry or better, ESFR sprinklers, 30 ft or lower storage.
- Maximum without referral: **$40M** TIV per building.

## Referral / decline

- Referral: plastic commodities in racks above 25 ft, or tenant-controlled
  hazards unknown to the named insured.
- Decline: frame construction over $10M in a CAT zone.
