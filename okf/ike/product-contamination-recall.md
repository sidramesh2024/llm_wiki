# IKE-UW-240 Product contamination and recall

## Appetite

- Accidental contamination and government-recall sublimits available to
  food occupancies that maintain a written recall plan and mock-recall log.
- Maximum recall sublimit without referral: **$5M**.

## Referral

- Brands with national distribution and no crisis-communications vendor.
- Prior FDA warning letter in the last 36 months.
