# IKE-UW-330 Puerto Rico CAT

## Rule

- All Puerto Rico property is referral.
- Named-storm deductible 5% of TIV; earthquake sublimit required.
- Book accumulation cap for Puerto Rico: **$80M** (see
  [Puerto Rico accumulation](../exposure/accum-puerto-rico.md)).
