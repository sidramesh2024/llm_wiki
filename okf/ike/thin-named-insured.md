# IKE-UW-410 Thin named insured

Applies when the applicant is an LLC or holdco formed in the last 24 months,
or when operating history sits in a recently acquired target.

## Rule

- Automatic referral.
- Require three years of target loss runs, not the LLC's blank history.
- Additional named insureds and mortgagees must be scheduled.
- Decline if no loss runs and TIV exceeds $25M.
